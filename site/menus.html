The menus

The menu is built for a controller on a television: no mouse, a legend of button glyphs on every screen, and layouts that follow the console's 4:3, 16:9 and 720p outputs.

What it is made of

  • The main menu is the mainui_cpp menu (our fork, in 3rdparty/mainui). It already moves a single cursor with a gamepad; the fork adds the Xbox screens, the legend bar and the fonts.
  • The console and the HUD are drawn by the engine itself. All menu layers draw through the same 2D primitives of the NV2A renderer, so the renderer's 2D pass is what makes any menu appear.
  • FreeVGUI, a reimplementation of Half-Life's GUI library, is a separate layer. Single-player Half-Life does not need it. It is planned for multiplayer panels (scoreboard, message of the day, team menus).

Why this is more than a port of the menu

  • Navigation without a mouse. The cursor skips items that are greyed out or mouse-only, and the order the controls are registered in is the order the pad visits them, so each screen is checked against that. Two screens had it wrong and were fixed. A button legend (A, B, X, Y glyphs and what they do) is drawn at the bottom of every screen.
  • Widescreen and 720p. When the output is 16:9 the engine reports 854x480, so the field of view, the HUD and the menu all widen instead of stretching. The menu scales to the screen's height and the HUD anchors to its edges. r3d_vid_aspect forces 4:3 or 16:9 and r3d_vid_mode 1 selects 1280x720, which needs an HDTV pack with 720p allowed in the dashboard.
  • Memory. The menu releases its fonts and background while you play and loads them back when the menu opens. The backgrounds are baked offline into tiles, with a compressed variant that cuts the menu's textures to about a third.
  • Fonts and text. Fira Sans Condensed, because the design's first choice lacked Cyrillic and some Central European letters. Strings are looked up by their English text, and a German translation ships as a proof.
  • Look. An amber palette from the menu design, shipped as a data file; a focused button glows.
  • Testing without a console. Scripts drive the menus in the emulator with a virtual controller (tools/xemu-pad.py and friends).
  • Leaving the game. Start, Back and both triggers together return to the dashboard.

Still to do

  • An on-screen keyboard (the hook exists; the window is not written).
  • The FreeVGUI backend and gamepad navigation by widget position, for multiplayer panels.
  • Multiplayer screens, and VGUI2 for games that need it (not before Half-Life runs well on hardware).
  • A speaker-output row (stereo, mono, surround) once the console's setting layout is understood.
  • A font with a licence that covers the small help text.

The design notes are docs/r3d/ui-architecture.md, and each change is in CHANGELOG.md and the divergence ledger.
