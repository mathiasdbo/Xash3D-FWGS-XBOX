Black Mesa Inbound to Unforeseen Consequences, on a real Xbox

The same route as the xemu session, played on a retail Original Xbox with every retail map archive on its hard drive. A probe in the renderer recorded every frame's time and the free memory at each level change, and saved them to the drive for collection over FTP afterwards.

44 fps overall, against 60 in xemu

xemu ran every map but one at the 60 Hz cap, so it hides the console's real cost. On the hardware only c0a0e, c1a0d and the brief c1a0 revisit hold 60; the tram ride runs near 50.

Four maps run near 30 fps

c0a0c (31), c0a0d (31), c1a0e (34) and c1a0c (30) spend most frames at 31-36 ms: the large, open views of the tram's last stretch and of the test chamber and its aftermath. These are the maps to profile first, CPU against GPU.

No vsync steps

Frame times vary smoothly (many at 20 ms, 50 fps) instead of snapping from 16.7 to 33.3 ms, so the renderer does not wait on vsync here. A dropped frame costs a few milliseconds, not half the frame rate.

Memory matches xemu

23.3-26.4 MiB free at each map start, the largest block equal to free every time, revisits back at the same figures, and within 1.4 MiB of xemu map for map. Level changes take 1.7-4.1 s. The same long single frames appear (254 ms on c0a0e, 412-437 ms on c1a0e/c1a0c).
