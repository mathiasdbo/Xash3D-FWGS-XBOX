Black Mesa Inbound to Unforeseen Consequences

One hand-played session on the Xbox build with every retail map archive on the disc. Every frame's time was recorded by a probe in the renderer, and free memory was sampled at each level change.

Locked to 60 fps

Every map averages 59.5–60.2 fps except c1a0b. Frames sit on the display's 16.7 ms refresh; the probe's "half the frames over 16.7 ms" split was timer jitter either side of that line, not a 30 fps mode.

c1a0b is the slow one

54 fps average, 1% low at 29 fps, and 412 frames over 20 ms: the renderer falls behind vsync in parts of that map. It is the only map worth profiling from this run.

A few long hitches

Single frames of 261 ms (c0a0e), 416 ms (c1a0e) and 347 ms (c1a0c) during play. Level changes take 2.0–3.9 s. None repeats, which fits one-off events such as autosaves or first-time sound loads.

Memory holds across changes

Free memory at each map start stays between 22.3 and 26.6 MiB over 13 changes, the largest contiguous block always equals free memory (no fragmentation), and revisits of c1a0/c1a0d come back to the same figures.
